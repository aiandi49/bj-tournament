Table VII — Rules Guide

Touch a card once it's been dealt and you are disqualified from this session — no warnings, no exceptions. If a card lands off the table, only the dealer retrieves it.

How you win

You're playing against each other, not the house. Beat the dealer's hand to grow your bank. Whoever holds the largest bank when the tournament ends takes the title.

Blackjack & splits

A natural blackjack pays 3× your bet, paid the instant it's revealed — the dealer never checks for blackjack first. You can split any two cards, not just a matching pair — an Ace and a 3 can be split just as legally as two 8s. Any hand that reaches 21 after a split also pays 3×, no matter how it got there. A winning hand's chips move to the center of the betting box to show the seat is settled for the round.

The dealer's hand

The dealer's first card is dealt face down and stays hidden until every player has acted. The second card is dealt face up.

Betting order & the button

The button marks the seat that acts first this round. It rotates one seat to the left after every round.

Round one — the temperature check

Every table opens with a flat $1 minimum bet for everyone, regardless of the blind schedule. If escalating blinds are switched off, the table stays at a $1 minimum all night.

Chip counts

You may request a full table chip count a limited number of times. It shows chip colors only, stacked so no one can count an exact total — requesting one pauses the blind clock.

Sitting out

You can sit out a round. The dealer still collects that round's table minimum from your stack either way — sitting out skips your cards, not the toll.

At the table

  • Phones only — no other devices at your seat.
  • No cursing, no fighting — either one removes you from the session immediately.
  • Seven seconds to bet once it's your turn, or the table minimum is placed for you.

Practice mode

Practice hands against the computer are free, always. Use them to learn the betting flow before playing for a bank.

Tournament mode

A seated tournament always requires a dealer, live or app-hosted — the dealer's cut is built into the buy-in the same way it is at a physical table.

Head-to-head mode

One-on-one play carries a higher entry: $35 goes to the dealer's cut, with a $50 minimum total buy-in, and first place doubles the entry fee back.

Accounts & verification

Any mode involving real money requires an account, identity verification, and an explicit on-record agreement to these rules before your first hand. This app does not currently implement real-money settlement — that requires a licensed gaming operator and legal review before launch.
